## 📖 Overview

**KuralGuard** is an AI-assisted civic safety platform designed to empower students to report hazards and make their local and campus communities safer. From a single local observation, KuralGuard bridges the gap between **incident reporting**, **AI-driven risk analysis**, and **community action**.

🎥 **Watch the full walkthrough:** [KuralGuard Demo on YouTube](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VAnNPpK7Zlc)

## ✨ Key Features

### 📝 1. Smart Concern Reporting
* **Structured Incident Logging:** Students can easily select an issue category, tag the exact location, describe the hazard, and set urgency levels.
* **Evidence Attachment:** Upload photos or supporting details to validate safety concerns.
* **Anonymous Reporting:** Built-in privacy option allowing students to report sensitive issues safely without fear.

### 🧠 2. AI-Powered Risk Analysis & Brief Generation
* **Automated Risk Assessment:** Analyzes incoming reports to classify risk severity (e.g., identifying high-risk road safety hazards near school zones).
* **Contributing Factor Breakdown:** Highlights underlying causes automatically—such as high-speed traffic, poor street lighting, or missing zebra crossings.
* **Immediate Safety Recommendations:** Delivers instant, practical precautions for students (e.g., avoiding solo crossings and using supervised points).
* **Actionable Civic Proposals:** Generates structured recommendations for local authorities and community leaders (e.g., installing speed-calming measures, proper lighting, and crosswalks).
* **Multilingual Support:** Transforms local observations into accessible, multilingual safety briefs so everyone in the community stays informed.

### 📊 3. Community Safety Priority Dashboard
* **Live Safety Pulse:** View real-time updates on active safety incidents across the community.
* **Risk & Resolution Tracking:** Monitor incident risk levels, ongoing interventions, and resolution statuses in one centralized view.

### 🆘 4. Centralized Safety Resources Hub
* Quick, one-stop access to verified support channels, including:
  * 🚨 **Emergency Helplines**
  * 👩 **Women’s Safety Support**
  * 🏥 **Medical Assistance**
  * 🤝 **Trusted Community Support Services**
